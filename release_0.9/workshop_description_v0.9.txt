In Skaven society there is no such thing as a "friend" - only temporary allies who haven't betrayed-backstabbed you YET.

This mod deals with the funny concept of "friendly fire": it simply lets Skaven shoot-aim straight through their own units, yes-yes. The AI does it too.

In case you want to keep some of your allies a little longer: in the glorious Mod Configuration Tool you can choose who gets shot through - expendable units (Skavenslaves and Pusbags, default), Clanrats, all infantry, monster infantry, or all units (lords & monsters included).

Mind you, it is not balanced and never will be. Your units will take damage and will rout.

Other mods do a similar thing, but they mostly raise the threshold at which ranged units are allowed to shoot. In this one, the selected units are simply ignored when the game checks for friendly obstacles.

REQUIRED: memreader Plus - https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3811098873
OPTIONAL: Mod Configuration Tool, for the options above. Without it, only expendable units are shot through.

After a game update the mod may switch itself off until I update it. It tells you once in the main menu and changes nothing else.
